How it works Source
Loading the runtime… Nothing leaves this tab. Your files stay in memory; your rules are saved in this browser only. Once the page has loaded you can disconnect from the network and it still works.

01 Statements

CSV exports from online banking — every account you spend from, cards included. PDF statements from CIBC, RBC, Scotiabank and ScotiaLine are converted here in the tab and refused unless they add up to the totals the statement prints.

    Eight months, four accounts, nobody's real money.

    02 What only you know

    Which transfers are your own accounts, who your employer is, what you have lent and to whom. Patterns are case-insensitive regular expressions; first match wins.

    Saved in this browser as you type.

    03 Run

    Drop statements and press Build the budget.
