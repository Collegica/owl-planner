# State what the figures are and are not, on every output surface

Arrive Finance labels every output "personalized financial education, not financial advice";
FUNDerelele calls its holdings and gains "estimates for personal tracking". The line matters
less for liability than for the reader's stance: it tells them what they are holding. OWL
Planner's README says it; `budget.md` and the console do not.

A one-line footer on `budget.md`, the console headline and the ratio profile: measured from
your own statements over N complete months; not advice; verify limits and rates against the
tax authority. It buys the right framing at the moment of use; it costs a sentence.
