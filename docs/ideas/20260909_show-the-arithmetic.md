# The report shows the months behind every headline number

Retire, Eh? states "the math is always visible" as a principle and means it: every projection
is accompanied by its calculation. OWL Planner prints `RECURRING $X/month` and the reader has
to trust it — the question "is that an estimate?" was asked precisely because the number
arrived without its derivation.

Under RECURRING, list the complete months and their totals with the median marked; under each
SET ASIDE line, the count of transactions; under LEVELLED, the transfers that were levelled.
`budget.md` gets the same as an appendix. It buys a report that argues rather than asserts; it
costs a few lines of output.
