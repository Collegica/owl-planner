# A tax and benefit engine inside OWL Planner — retired

The retirement toolkit encodes federal and provincial tax, OAS clawback, RRIF wind-up and a
Monte Carlo drawdown. That is what a plan needs after `$X`, and it is retired here because it
is somebody else's tool: OWL Planner measures a year of spending and hands off. The hand-off
(IDEA-0011) is the idea; the engine is the thing that would turn a 800-line tool into a
maintenance obligation against every budget.
